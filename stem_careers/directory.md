# Directory of STEM Careers in Kentucky

The following is a list of resources for STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) carrers.
The resources are intended for students that plan to study or work in Kentucky.
I have intentionally used an expansive definition of STEM to include fields like medicine, education, and agriculture.

### Science Teacher
* [KCTCS Education - Associate in Applied Science](https://catalog.kctcs.edu/programs-of-study/aas/education/education-aas/) - 
This degree will not qualify you to teach.
You will need to complete a four-year degree.
It is a good way to start an education program
* [Morehead State Univiersity MSUTeach Program](https://www.moreheadstate.edu/academics/colleges/education/msuteach/)
A teacher certification program for science majors.
A good option for people looking for flexibility in their career path.
